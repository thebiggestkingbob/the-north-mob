01 / The origin

From Ga-Rankuwa, Pretoria.
Into the conversation.

THE NORTH MOB is part of the conversation around breakout South African hip-hop and Pretoria’s evolving sound.

From Ga-Rankuwa, Pretoria, the collective has built an identity around its music, collaborators and its blend of trap and Bacardi influences. Its H4B project is presented as having passed 1 million streams across Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube (artist-supplied album figure) — an artist-supplied claim that must be independently substantiated before external investor circulation.

That momentum creates a practical opening: instead of treating attention as the final result, make every music video, performance, appearance, photograph, social post and documentary episode an opportunity to send fans toward a product, wishlist, community or live experience.

Why now? The audience already has a reason to pay attention. The late-2026 video and December performance calendar offer an immediate window to seed exclusive clothing before the first commercial drops, then take the activation into schools, campuses and the first MOBTIE$ event in March 2027.

02 / The thesis
Music creates attention. Clothing makes it tangible. Documentation compounds attention. MOBTIE$ creates an experience fans can attend.

MERCH

Products give fans something to own, wear, gift and share. Purchases are tracked through the THE NORTH MOB online shop.

MOB VLOGS

An independent documentation channel, regularly collaborating with the group's main channels. Equipment is purchased once; everyday production stays in-house.

MOBTIE$

Three organic events a year — March, August and November — selling R100 tickets, food, drinks and merchandise.

These three activities reinforce each other. The investor funds the wider activation, but receives a share of merchandise profit only. Event tickets, food and drinks, documentation income, music, bookings and endorsements are excluded from the investor’s participation.

03 / The products

Not a logo on a shirt.
A physical identity.

Exclusive prototypes go first to THE NORTH MOB, the rollout crew and carefully chosen collaborators. Their presence in music videos, gigs and documentary footage builds familiarity before the public release. The THE NORTH MOB website launches its merchandise wishlist and size registration in December 2026. All catalogue products become available by the March 2027 MOBTIE$ event, even when produced in small quantities or via preorder.

04 / Launch sequence

Make every appearance
a call to action.

NOVEMBER 2026

Seed, don't sell

  • Secure the initial R250,000 tranche and approve the production schedule.
  • Manufacture exclusive prototypes for THE NORTH MOB and the rollout crew.
  • Gift selected H4B featured artists and culturally relevant collaborators.
  • Film the “Peke, Foshsol” music video featuring the merchandise without announcing a public launch.
  • Produce campaign photography and begin MOB VLOGS documentation using purchased equipment.
DECEMBER 2026

Turn visibility into demand

  • THE NORTH MOB wears the exclusive garments at December gigs and appearances.
  • Launch the THE NORTH MOB website merchandise wishlist with the full catalogue visible.
  • Let fans like products, submit preferred sizes and register interest; use this to prioritise production.
  • Launch the first three purchasable apparel products approximately 10–11 days before New Year.
  • Keep permanent merchandise objects available independently of the quarterly clothing-drop rhythm.
JAN–FEB 2027

Bring it to the audience

  • Run permission-based high-school and university appearances, performances and giveaways.
  • Wear the merchandise, document the activation and direct fans to the THE NORTH MOB online shop.
  • Roll out the remaining catalogue in small batches, based on wishlist signals and production capacity.
  • Publish consistent MOB VLOGS episodes and collaborate with the main THE NORTH MOB channel.
MARCH 2027

Everything is live

  • Hold the first MOBTIE$ event, targeting 300–500 attendees.
  • Make every merchandise design available for purchase or clearly disclosed preorder, even without full-scale stock.
  • Sell R100 tickets, own food and drinks, and merchandise at the event.
  • Capture event footage, conversion data and inventory sell-through.
APRIL 2027–2031

Repeat what works

  • Continue MOBTIE$ every March, August and November.
  • Target three new apparel designs per quarter, subject to demand; maintain the objects catalogue.
  • Reinvest in proven products and adjust batch sizes based on cash and sales.
  • Expand reach through ongoing videos, appearances, community activations and collaboration.

07 / Preferred return

Reward the risk.
Then share the scale.

The investor provides R500,000 in two R250,000 tranches to activate the whole ecosystem, but participates in merchandise distributable profit only. The return is a cash-distribution waterfall, not a claim on music, image, performances, events or THE BIGGEST's independent operations.

40%

Recovery stage

Investor receives 40% of distributable merchandise profit until cumulative investor distributions reach R500,000.

25%

Growth stage

Investor receives 25% of distributable merchandise profit from R500,000 until cumulative distributions reach R2,000,000.

5%

Scale stage

Once cumulative investor distributions reach R2,000,000, the investor receives 5% of future distributable merchandise profit, without a fixed expiry.

R2 million is the cumulative investor payout hurdle, not a guaranteed payment or business valuation. The 5% tail continues indefinitely unless the parties negotiate a buyout or termination mechanism. If a year's distribution crosses a hurdle, that year's profit is split into the appropriate rate bands rather than applying one rate to the whole year.

Inside the investor's participation

  • Online merchandise and physical objects
  • Merchandise sold at MOBTIE$
  • Merchandise sold at other activations and appearances

Outside the investor's participation

  • THE NORTH MOB music, streaming, performance fees, endorsements and image rights
  • THE BIGGEST's existing and independent business
  • MOB VLOGS revenue and MOBTIE$ ticket, food and drink revenue

The parties should agree to auditable merchandise accounts, cost allocation, reporting and distribution timing. A perpetual 5% share is a material long-term obligation and should be legally reviewed alongside optional buyout rights.
